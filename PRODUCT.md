# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Egyptian parents evaluating practical coding, AI, and entrepreneurship programs for kids and teens aged 6 to 17 in Alexandria (68 Fouad St) and online across Egypt. Secondary usage covers any multi-level coding/STEM academy adopting the reusable `academy-stage-3d` MaxP storefront template.

## Product Purpose

Help parents immediately understand which track matches their child's age and experience across a 15-level progression (`Code · Startup · Pitch`), feel confident in real hands-on outcomes, and book a free evaluation session (`جلسة تقييم مجانية`) or explore tracks in seconds.

## Positioning

Unlike fragmented single-course coding camps or generic e-commerce templates, the academy unites engineering craft (`Level 1` Scratch / Code.org / MIT App Inventor / PictoBlox AI / Microbit / Web & JS -> `Level 15` Python Pro) with real student startup building and live Demo Day pitching (`Code · Startup · Pitch`).

## Operating Context

Parents often browse on mobile phones (`390px`) or desktop (`1440px`) in Arabic (RTL), comparing age brackets (`6-9`, `10-13`, `14-17`), tuition offers (`15%` sibling discount, `EARLY10` early registration code), and branch/online logistics (`68 Fouad St, Alexandria` + Online + WhatsApp `01041510027`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must stay fully themeable as reusable MaxP storefront template `academy-stage-3d` while showcasing CodeTreps (`codetreps@maxp.com` / `https://codetreps.maxp.online`).
- Arabic-first RTL layout (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`, `IBM Plex Mono` for numeric telemetry/codes/prices, `letter-spacing: 0` on Arabic).
- Strictly zero invented testimonials, student statistics, or fake customer faces; custom studio visual plates focus on physical hardware, coding environments, robotics/Microbit kits, and pitch/workbench scenes alongside CodeTreps' real brand logo and real catalog data.
- Interactive age/level explorer and direct free evaluation booking flow live cleanly inside MaxP storefront architecture (no console errors, mobile 85%+ viewport utilization, zero blast radius on other themes).

## Product Principles

1. **Outcome Clarity Over Tech Hype:** Parents want to see what a 7-year-old vs a 15-year-old actually builds and pitches, not generic AI clichés.
2. **Age & Level Immediately Actionable:** Choosing age/level and booking a free evaluation session belongs front and center as interactive utility, not buried under marketing paragraphs.
3. **Tactile Craft Reflects Hardware & Code:** Surfaces, motion, and typography must feel engineered—like real hardware kits, notebooks, and demo-day craft rather than generic SaaS dark boxes.
4. **Template Isolation & Arabic Typographic Discipline:** Arabic RTL legibility and MaxP storefront theme architecture govern every section without affecting any other merchant template.
